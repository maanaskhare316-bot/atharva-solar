Install a residential rooftop solar system for your home and explore the direct financial incentives under current central government schemes.

Optimal for heavy consumption with air conditioning. Maximum subsidy allowed per residential connection.

Eligibility & Required Documents

  • Residential Ownership: Individual homeowners with a valid domestic DISCOM electricity connection in MP.
  • Electricity Bill: Latest electricity bill in the name of the applicant.
  • Identity & Address: Aadhaar Card linked with electricity consumer number and bank account.
  • Bank Account: Nationalized bank account passbook / cancelled cheque for DBT subsidy credit.
  • DCR Solar Modules: Must use Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) ALMM-approved panels.

Step-by-Step Subsidy Process

  1. Registration on National Portal: Application submission on the PM Surya Ghar national portal.
  2. Technical Feasibility Approval: DISCOM examines connection capacity and approves load synchronization.
  3. Turnkey Installation by Atharva: Authorized PWD & Electrical execution with certified DCR panels.
  4. Net-Meter Inspection & CEIG: DISCOM engineers install the bidirectional net meter.
  5. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): Government disburses subsidy up to ₹78,000 directly to customer's bank account.
IMPORTANT REGULATORY DISCLAIMER

Subsidy is subject to government scheme guidelines, eligibility, approved system capacity, installation requirements and successful application approval. The amount of up to ₹78,000 is not guaranteed for every customer. Verify current scheme rules before applying.
