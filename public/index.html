About Atharva Enterprises

Your Trusted Partner in the Solar Energy Journey.

Atharva Enterprises is an authorized A Class Electrical & PWD Contractor based in Madhya Pradesh, delivering turnkey solar power systems, solar panel supplies, and comprehensive electrical contracting.

We bring rigorous engineering discipline to every solar project. Whether powering an urban rooftop bungalow, an agricultural cold storage facility, or a large industrial manufacturing plant, our solutions are designed to deliver optimal kilowatt-hour yield under intense Indian weather conditions.

  • No Compromise on Safety: Heavy-gauge galvanized iron structures, dual chemical earthing, and Class-A lightning protection.
  • Government Subsidy Facilitation: Seamless registration and documentation assistance under Central PM Surya Ghar schemes.
  • Transparent Quotations: Detailed engineering line-item specifications with zero hidden charges.
